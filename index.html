How Fights End Across the UFC

Examining knockout share by weight division, year-to-year stoppage swings, and where UFC fighters come from.

By Xavier J. Serrano | Published Nov. 24, 2026

KO/TKO Finish Share Drops Across Lighter UFC Weight Classes

Heavyweight leads all UFC divisions in knockout and TKO finishes (52.07%), while lighter men's divisions and women's weight classes see a smaller share of strike stoppages, down to 13.92% at Women's Strawweight.

Source: Fightomic (UFC finish rates by weight class).

Finish Rate Hits A 5-Year High Following 2024 Low Finish Rates

After dipping in 2024, the share of UFC bouts ending by stoppage rebounded in 2025 and reached a five-year high in 2026, showing how finish frequency shifts from year to year.

Source: Fightomic (UFC annual stoppage rate trends, 2022–2026).

Where UFC Fighters Are Born Across The Globe

Mapping UFC fighter birthplaces highlights how roster talent clusters in hubs like the United States (1,776) and Brazil (407) alongside international pipelines across Europe, Asia, Oceania, and the Americas.
